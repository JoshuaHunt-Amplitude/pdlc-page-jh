The new bottleneck: knowing what to build

Quality comes from iteration. The military strategist John BoydJohn Boyd · 1927–1997USAF fighter pilot and Pentagon consultant. His theories on decision cycles shaped military, business, and legal strategy worldwide. — via Wikipedia ↗ showed that whoever cycles their OODA loopObserve, Orient, Decide, ActDecision-making as an iterative cycle. Influential across litigation, business, law enforcement, military strategy, and cybersecurity. — via Wikipedia ↗ fastest wins the dogfight. Whether it's a team of 1,000 or a solo developer - every product builder runs through roughly the same loop to build and iterate on a product: build, ship, use, learn.

Coding agents are collapsing build to zero. But use and learn still crawl by hand. Someone manually combs the data for signals, decides what's worth building, proves if the change worked, and reflects on the process.

Knowing the right thing to ship is more important than ever.

Your next best move is already in your data

Every click, session replay, customer feedback, log, and agent trace is a signal about what's working and what's not — and your next best idea is buried in them.

A self-improving product continuously analyzes every signal, corroborates evidence across sources, and turns that context into Opportunities to improve and grow.

Then it tracks whether the ship actually moved the metric and remembers the result, so every cycle is sharper than the last.

Introducing

The world's first
self-improving product engine

A self-improving product runs the whole loop on its own.

It reads the signals across your product, decides what's worth building, and orchestrates the people and agents who ship it. Then it measures what worked (monitoring outcomes, running experiments) and feeds the learning into the next cycle.

So your product doesn't just grow, it compounds: every loop sharper than the last.

The loop that's always running

Wave knows what to build

A proactive product agent that helps teams build self-improving products.

It continuously analyzes your Amplitude data for Signals, surfaces Opportunities you or your agents can approve and ship, and tracks the experiments and Outcomes, so your product learns what works and gets smarter every cycle.

It surfaced hundreds of Opportunities. We've shipped better and faster than ever.
