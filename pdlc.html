Inside the System

How the loops build on each other.

Four loops. Four time scales. Each one feeds the next. As you scroll, watch the system build from the inside out.

00
The Innermost Loop
Your product heals itself.

The fastest loop in the system requires zero human judgment. It watches system health — error rates, latency, crash patterns — and responds in milliseconds. When something breaks, the system diagnoses and patches it before any user knows it happened. No ticket filed. No standup called. The product handled it.

01
The Behavioral Loop
Your product watches what users stop doing.

Not every problem throws an exception. A drop in activation, a funnel everyone's abandoning, a feature nobody's using — these are behavioral signals, not system errors. The Fix loop watches user behavior continuously, surfaces anomalies before they compound, and routes the right signal to the right person. Human judgment enters only at triage.

The Strategy Loop
Your roadmap runs on evidence.

The Decide loop is where human judgment meets machine speed. Usage patterns surface competing hypotheses. The machine models both paths, recommends the higher-impact bet, and ships it with the hypothesis attached. Real outcome data validates the call in weeks — not quarters. The team decides. Data confirms who was right.

The Strategic Loop
Your strategy gets smarter every cycle.

The outermost loop synthesizes everything the inner loops have learned. Resolved failures. Behavioral patterns. Validated experiments. All of it rolls up into a clear picture of where the product is headed — updated every quarter by real outcomes, not assumptions. Strategy stops being a slide deck and starts being a compounding institutional knowledge base.

The compounding

Cut a loop, and the system stops.

Without the inner loops, your product breaks in silence. Without the outer loops, your product gets better at the wrong thing. The magic isn't any single loop — it's the feedback between them. Outer loops recalibrate what the inner loops chase. Inner loops give the outer loops something real to reason from.
