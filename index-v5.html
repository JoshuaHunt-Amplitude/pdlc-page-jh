Why PDLC

Deciding what to build is the new bottleneck.

Every team runs the same loop: build → ship → use → learn. Coding agents collapsed the build half — a PR that took a week now takes minutes.

The other half hasn't moved. Your team still digs through Slack threads, replays, and feedback to answer one question: what's worth building next? Engineers can ship faster than ever. Your roadmap can't keep up.

The teams pulling ahead built their own systems to close this gap. Stripe's "Minions" merge 1,300+ PRs/week. Ramp's Inspect authors 57% of merged PRs. Their devs aren't 10× engineers — they just always know what to point their agents at.

PDLC reads the product data Amplitude already owns, tells your team what matters, drafts the plan, and tracks the outcome. People still decide what ships.

Same team. 2–3× the throughput. Same review bar.
