Manifesto // Loop 4.0

Build · Ship
Use · Learn

Every product team runs the same loop: build → ship → use → learn. After fifty years of progress — and now AI coding — the build half is instant. The use → learn half still sits in the stone ages.

Understanding what people want is the limiting reagent. And nobody can tell you what they want — "if I had asked people what they wanted, they would have said faster horses." So look at what people do. Behavior reveals intent.
