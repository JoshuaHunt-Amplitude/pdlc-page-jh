The false summit

The new bottleneck: knowing what to build.

Quality comes from iteration — the strategist John Boyd showed that whoever cycles their OODA loop fastest wins the dogfight. Every product runs the same loop: build, ship, use, learn.

Coding agents are collapsing build to zero. But use and learn still crawl by hand — someone manually combs the data for signals, decides what's worth building, proves if the change worked, and reflects on the process.

Shipping stopped being the bottleneck. Knowing what to build is.

Products can improve themselves now.

A self-improving product runs the whole loop on its own.

It reads the signals across your product, decides what's worth building, and orchestrates the people and agents who ship it. Then it measures what worked — monitoring outcomes, running experiments — and feeds the learning into the next cycle.

So your product doesn't just grow, it compounds: every loop sharper than the last.

The signal

Your product already signals what to build.

Every click, session replay, customer feedback, log, and agent trace is a signal — and your next best idea is buried in them.

Most of it goes unread. A self-improving product continuously analyzes every signal, corroborates evidence across sources, and turns that context into opportunities to improve and grow.

Meet Wave

Wave knows what to build.

A proactive product agent that helps teams build self-improving products.

It continuously analyzes your Amplitude data for Signals, surfaces Opportunities you or your agents can approve and ship, and tracks the experiments and Outcomes — so your product learns what works and gets smarter every cycle.

Proof — we run it on ourselves

We use Wave to build Amplitude.

For months, Wave has run on Amplitude itself — auto-triaging bugs, optimizing our landing pages and product flows, and sharpening our own agents and tools by analyzing their traces.

It surfaced hundreds of Opportunities — and we've shipped better and faster than ever.
